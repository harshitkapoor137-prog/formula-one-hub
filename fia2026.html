FIA 2026 Regulations

Powering the future of Formula 1

What is changing in 2026?

The 2026 Formula 1 regulations represent the biggest technical reset in modern F1 history. Introduced by the FIA, these rules focus on sustainability, closer racing, and future-ready technology while maintaining extreme performance.


What’s New for 2026?

New Power Unit

The 2026 engines will feature a 50/50 power split between the internal combustion engine and electric energy. The MGU-H will be removed, while the MGU-K output will be significantly increased.

  • 100% sustainable fuels
  • Increased electric power output
  • Simpler, cheaper engine design

Active Aerodynamics

Cars will feature active aerodynamic elements that adjust on straights and corners. This reduces drag on straights and increases downforce during braking and cornering.

  • Front and rear wing movement
  • Lower drag on straights
  • Better overtaking opportunities

Smaller & Lighter Cars

2026 cars will be shorter, narrower, and lighter, making them more agile and improving racing quality.

  • Reduced wheelbase
  • Lower minimum weight
  • Improved handling

Sustainability Focus

Sustainability is central to the 2026 vision, with cleaner fuels and reduced environmental impact without sacrificing performance.

  • Net-zero carbon fuels
  • Lower fuel consumption
  • Road-relevant hybrid technology

Why the 2026 rules matter

These regulations ensure Formula 1 remains the pinnacle of motorsport while aligning with global sustainability goals. Faster, lighter cars combined with cleaner energy will define a new era of racing, attracting new manufacturers and improving competition.
