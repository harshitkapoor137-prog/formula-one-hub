FIA

The governing body of Formula 1

What is the FIA?

The Fédération Internationale de l’Automobile (FIA) is the global governing body of motorsport and the federation of the world’s leading motoring organisations. Founded in 1904, the FIA is responsible for regulating Formula 1 and ensuring safety, fairness, and competition integrity.

What does the FIA do?

  • Creates and enforces Formula 1 technical regulations
  • Oversees race operations and stewarding
  • Ensures driver and circuit safety
  • Issues Super Licences to drivers
  • Manages cost cap and sporting rules

How does the FIA function?

The FIA works closely with Formula One Management (FOM), teams, and race officials. Race stewards apply rules during Grand Prix weekends, while technical delegates inspect cars for compliance. The FIA also leads innovation in sustainability and safety across global motorsport.
